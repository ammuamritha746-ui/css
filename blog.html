Author: Amritha

The responsible use and conservation of natural resources are vital for maintaining ecological balance and ensuring a sustainable future. Overexploitation, pollution, deforestation, and climate change have led to the depletion of many valuable resources, threatening biodiversity and human livelihoods. Governments, organizations, and individuals all have a role to play in protecting natural resources by reducing waste, recycling materials, planting trees, conserving water and energy, and adopting environmentally friendly practices. By using natural resources wisely, we can preserve them for future generations while supporting sustainable development.

Natural resources are not a gift from our ancestors; they are a loan from our children.
